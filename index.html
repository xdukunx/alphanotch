AlphaNotch

A personal desktop setup for Windows: a status bar (YASB) and a notch-style island (Coucou) working together.

AlphaNotch shows what is going on in the background (music, timers, downloads, to-dos) in the island at the top of the screen. It can optionally sync your Google Tasks list with the to-do card in the island and show today's Google Calendar events.

It is a personal, non-commercial project made by one developer. It has no server and no account system.
