Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

AlphaNotch is a personal desktop application for Windows. This policy explains what it does with your data.

What AlphaNotch accesses

If you choose to connect your Google account, AlphaNotch asks for one permission: to view and manage your Google Tasks (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks). It uses it only to read your task lists and to create, edit, complete and delete tasks that you change inside the app, so the to-do card on your desktop matches your Google Tasks.

Where your data goes

  • Your tasks are fetched directly from Google to your own computer. AlphaNotch has no server and the developer never receives your data.
  • The sign-in token is stored on your computer in the Windows Credential Manager. It is never written to a plain file and never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • AlphaNotch does not collect analytics or telemetry, does not sell data and does not share data with third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AlphaNotch's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can disconnect at any time from the app, and you can revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing the app also removes the stored token from your Windows Credential Manager.
